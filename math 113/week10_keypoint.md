(when lim x->a, the interval is open interval including a, lim x->a+, (a,b), lim x->a- : (c,a), lim x-> infinity : (m,infinity)

lim x->a+,a-,infinity,-infinity,a f(x) =0, lim g(x) = 0 or lim f(x) = +-infinity, lim g(x) = +-, -+infinity,

right side's limit value exists or +-infinity,

lim f(x)/g(x) = lim f'(x)/g'(x)

ex) lim x->1 lnx/(x-1) -> (1/x)/1 = 1
