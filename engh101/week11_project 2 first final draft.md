**introduction**

Recently, The importance of research about resolving distrust in the judiciary has been increasing

as public’s distrust has become increased by media reporting on controversial judicial outcomes. 

But, regardless of this situation, government doesn’t present appropriate or sustainable solutions for

resolving this, and publics just present emotional and non-logical ways which are not in accordance

with the fundamental essence of law. Also, solutions presented by academic field have limits

considering human’s inherent traits and psychological biases. This situation ultimately causes social

division and confusion and even social collapse in the most extreme and worst possible situation.

Recently, developing technologies, the new technological alternative, AI judge, has been presented

through media. But, the reality is that this, too, has not been properly or sufficiently discussed in our

society and academic field. So, purpose of this essay is to provide analysis to the public about

whether AI judge can be alternative way instead of human judge overcoming the inherent limit of the

human one. For evaluating AI judge’s usefulness, defining the essence of law is essential as it

becomes the objective standards and criteria for evaluation. The section that follows presents two

influential theories which explain the essence of law, and we will deal with these two both. Also,

ultimately, in the section that follows, it will be argued that AI judge can be the best alternative instead

of human judge although it is not perfect technology.

**The limitation of natural law theory for realizing the essence of law**

First representative theory for explaining the essence of law is Natural law theory. Main context of this

theory is to admit upper law than positive law. This upper law called as natural law. 

This theory became the basic evidence for punishing Nazi war criminal in Nuremberg Trials after World War ∥.

Ultimately, this theory connects to many opinions about integrating morality and law. 

Although this assertion seems to be nice and righteous, there are serious problems in this. 

The main weakness with this theory is that Natural law is not a specific clause, 

and moral criteria for judging incidents is different for each person.

If legal professionals considered natural law theory as the basis one, 

one of the law’s important elements, objectivity, is disappeared. 

This system is not different with kangaroo court.

Hart (1958) also criticises Natural law theory, mentioning intellectual confusion of the public.

According to Hart (1958), faith integrating law and morality can hide essence of the problem we face

and make intellectual confusion for public. 

Ultimately, public becomes the condition which can not divide law as it is and as it ought to be. 

If they excessively trust law, our society will be quietism condition which means there are no criticism about law. 

If they excessively distrust law, they will judge recent law is not law, and anarchism will be the dominant ideology in our society. 

In other words, contrary to intuitive belief, integrating law and morality can make our society confused and it is far from essence of law. 

Through these, we can know necessity of new theoretical approach for realizing ideal law.

**Legal positivism, perfect theory for realizing the essence of law**

Another theory for explaining the essence of law is legal positivism. 

Legal positivism is academic view which just admits positive law as law. 

The strong point of this theory is objectivity. 

It interprets law as just clause and prevents intervention of moral judgement which can distort the essence of law through subjectivity. 

In other words, this theory is exact opposite one to natural law theory and it completely divides these two ideologies.

Many analysts now argue that the strategy of legal positivism cannot reflect our society or make our society righteous, 

but this is an emotion-biased assertion. When more deeply analyzing this theory, we can know this theory means the essence of law. 

One of the thesis which explains this theory perfectly is H. L. A. (1958). 

Hart is one of the giant legal philosopher in 20th century and he is considered as representative scholar of 20th-century legal positivism. 

He presented this thesis in 1958 as separating theory and legal positivism were criticized by public due to impact of Nazi.

Nazi criminals asserted they just followed the German law at that time, so there are no evidences for punishing themselves. 

Although they were found guilty in Nuremberg Trials, public’s distrust about legal positivism remained the same. 

So, Hart was written this thesis for changing this atmosphere made by public and legal professionals, 

this is evaluated as prestigious thesis. 

The main concept is core and penumbra, admitting the minimum of natural law when establishing law for survival.

First, Hart asserts there are two parts in law. Core is the certain parts which can make judgement based on law. 

And, penumbra is the uncertain parts which can not make judgement instantly through law.

In this situation, human’s judgement is necessary. But, this is not application of morality. 

Judge just regards law as clause and makes judgements considering previous judicial precedents, 

the situation when the law is established etc. 

In other words, it means human’s judgement is just mechanical decision for resolving problems made by penumbra parts not moral decision. 

Second, Hart admits the minimum of natural law when establishing law. 

It is for our lives as we will be dangerous if the law can not guarantee our safe. 

But, this permission is applied only for the process of making law. 

And the situation when morality affect legal judgment after making law considered as distortion of essence of law. 

Also, Hart asserts moral judgement can be applied when making law even if that law is not related to our lives. 

But, this is only for the time when establishing law. Hart presents 2 advantages which legal positivism has. 

They are clarity and sharper foundation for moral criticism. These are related to negative things of natural law theory. 

First, clarity means it can prevent anarchism and quietism made by intellectual confusion. 

Thanks to this trait, public can separate law and morality and they can avoid intellectual confusion. 

Second is related to quietism. In other words, 

avoiding quietism through separating theory can increase public’s interest of law and can propose new bills based on moral values. 

These are contexts of Hart’s theory. Hart’s one is evaluated as one of the perfect application method of legal positivism to reality. 

However, all the previously mentioned methods suffer from some serious drawbacks in reality, 

such as human judge's inherent limitations., application other incorrect methods 

related to legal positivism to reality instead of correct methods such as Hart’s one. 

In the following section, we will deal with the problems in reality although legal positivism is the perfect theory towards the essence of law.

**The problems caused by human judges**

As it is mentioned in previous paragraph, there are many practical problems in reality 

although the theory is perfect academically. 

It is because there are numerous restrictions in reality to operate theory’s concept correctly. 

Among them, the representative things are human judge’s adoption of incorrect application method 

about legal positivism and their inherent limitation related to unconscious bias. 

Human judge’s incorrect adoption becomes the hot issues especially in USA. 

Detailed situation can be found in Collins and Komatsu (2025) and Wolf (2024). 

These two sources both criticize originalism as the incorrect methods. 

Originalism is the extreme version of legal positivism 

and it only consider the original legislative intent and the situation at the time of law's enactment. 

This application method is different with Hart’s method, 

Hart’s one is distinguished from originalism as that it considers not only the original legislative intent

but also previous judicial precedent etc. First, the problem of originalism presented in those two sources 

is that it can’t reflect current society enough and it just pursues regressive political agenda 

which can yoke our constitutional rights at more century years ago. 

In other words, it means only pursuing originalism is not appropriate for recent society 

as social agreement becomes different from time to time. 

One of the best examples is historical changes about women's suffrage.

Although laws about restricting women’s vote right is deleted, 

if the other law about different field is established at the time

when women’ vote right is restricted and it exists until now,

originalism which only considers the situation at the time of law’s enactment can be evaluated as outdated methods 

which can not reflect current society. Even, the key point is human judges are misusing this theory for justifying their judgements. 

Other authors question the usefulness of such an approach. 

Other authors (see Collins & Komatsu, 2025; Wolf, 2024) question the usefulness of originalism arguing 

that human judges are misusing this theory through historical distortion and 

specific conservative organization(Conservative Federalist Society) is misusing originalism 

for making biased judgements through conservative ideology. In other words, 

it means human judges not only adopt incorrect theories for realizing ideal law

but also are even misusing these theories. The limitation of this approach is also 

that human judges have an inherent limitation related to unconscious bias. 

It means human judges can have unconscious bias 

although they tried not to be biased when making judgements.

Actually, Irwin and Real (2011) analyzed whether people 

who have diverse jobs have unconscious bias through IAT test which can measure unconscious bias of people.

Test results were different with participant’s evaluation about themselves.

It is revealed that many people who consider they don’t have any bias actually have unconscious bias.

Even, it is also revealed that doctors have unconscious bias when administering medication to a patient.

This results suggests us that human in diverse jobs even judges can have unconscious bias

although they tried not to be biased. In other words, human judges can not only adopt incorrect

methods or misuse the theory but also can be biased unconsciously

even if they try hard not to be biased. We can consider AI judge as the alternative in this situation. 

So, in the following section, we will deal with the context about 

whether AI judge can be alternative instead of human judge overcoming human’s inevitable problems.

**An evaluation of AI judge as the alternatives instead of human judge**

When we analyze the problems made by human judges again, they are all based on human’s subjectivity. 

To delete subjectivity, AI is the best alternative as it is operated based on their algorithms. 

The fact that AI is operated based on their algorithm informs us the judgement is predictable 

and not subjective but also we can design its engine 

so that they follow the correct application methods which can realize the essence of law. 

These merits is that machine can only posses, it matches with the essence of law exactly. 

However, Critics question the ability of AI algorithms to provide unbiased judgements, 

as AI is not a perfect technology. But, unlike human, as AI is based on their algorithms, 

this problem can be resolved enough as technology advances. 

This is the most important difference between human and AI as AI is just based on their algorithms and

operates mechanically. Also, the way AI judge’s algorithm designs is important to prevent AI makes

judgements which are not matched with the essence of law. 

Although there will need discuss for making fair algorithm, considering the essence of law,

moderate theory based on legal positivism like Hart’s one will be appropriate instead of originalism.

Furthermore, recently, there presents new theory such as popular originalism. 

This is explained in detail in Donnelly (2025), 

popular originalism means integrating originalism and living constitutionalism 

which considers constitution as living organism and using successive filters when making judgement. 

Of course, this theory also can be misused in reality.
Another major criticism of Balkin's theory, made by Novak (2025), is that integrating theory can also
be misused in reality. In other words, it warns integrating theory can be also misused like originalism.
But, this problem can be resolved enough through AI technology as AI is based on only their
algorithms. From this, we can know AI can resolve many problems which can’t resolve before. Like
many theories which I mentioned before, their exists many realistic methods for realizing legal
positivism. To make perfect algorithm, there will need numerous legal scholars for realizing the
essence of law. Lastly, There need not only technical and academic perspectives but also social
factors for making AI judge not be misused. Javed and Li (2025) deals with this context in detail. This
thesis emphasizes our social role for making AI judge not be misused with simulation results from
short term to long term when adopting AI judge. Simulation results indicate AI judge will be helpful for
our society although it makes biased judgement in short-term. Also, it emphasizes necessity of
monitoring by media and public as AI is not perfect technology. This is meaningful as it verify the
possibility about AI judge will be helpful for our society and it suggests the necessity of our social role
as monitor. In other words, we should always remember AI judge is not perfect technology and we
should monitor them not to be misused by specific organization. Until now, we evaluated AI judge and
presented the ways AI judge can be used for realizing essence of law from technical, academic and
social perspectives. Overall, AI judge can be evaluated as the alternatives instead of human judge for
realizing the essence of law and it can resolve many problems which can not resolve before. If
technical, academic and social complement is achieved, I expect AI judge will be the best way for the
realization of an ideal legal system.
