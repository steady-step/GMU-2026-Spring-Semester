# # Guiding Questions for Synthesizing Process

| Stage | Prompt / Task | Guiding Questions | Student Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Framing the Topic** | What is the big picture? | - What issue or problem are your sources addressing?<br>- Why is it important—socially, culturally, academically?<br>- What patterns, debates, or questions emerge across your sources? | A1~A3 |
| **2. Understanding Sources** | What are the key points of each source? | - What is the central claim of each author?<br>- What evidence, examples, or frameworks do they use?<br>- How does each source relate to your topic or research question? |A4~A6 |
| **3. Making Connections** | How do your sources relate to each other? | **Agreement & Reinforcement:**<br>- Which sources support similar arguments?<br>- How do they reinforce or build on each other’s points?<br><br>**Contrast & Tension:**<br>- Are there disagreements in findings, perspectives, or tone?<br>- What are the implications of those differences?<br><br>**Insight & Gaps:**<br>- Do any sources provide new or surprising angles?<br>- Is there a gap, silence, or missing perspective across your sources?<br><br>**Cause & Effect or Progression:**<br>- Do the sources show a development of ideas over time?<br>- Do any of them explain causes while others offer effects or solutions?<br><br>**Contextual or Theoretical Links:**<br>- Are the authors drawing from similar theories or fields?<br>- Do they use different lenses (e.g., psychological vs. sociological)?<br>- How do these relationships help clarify or complicate your own thinking? | A7~A17|
| **4. Your Position & Analysis** | What argument will you make based on your synthesis? | - What’s your thesis or main claim?<br>- How does your view connect to, build on, or challenge what your sources are saying?<br>- What unique insight are you offering? |A18~A20 |
| **5. Organizing Themes** | How will you structure your synthesis essay? | - What main themes or subtopics (2 or 3) are emerging?<br>- How will you organize your body paragraphs around these connections? |A21~A22 |
| **6. Reflecting on Purpose** | Where DO YOU fit in the conversation? | - Why does this topic matter to you?<br>- What do you hope readers understand or reconsider?<br>- What further questions or directions might this research lead to? | A23~A25|
