## The limitation of natural law theory for realizing ideal law

    Natural theory is one of the representative theory for defining the essence of law. 
    
    Main context of this theory is to admit upper law than positive law. 
    
    This upper law called as natural law. 
    
    This theory became the basic evidence for punishing Nazi war criminal in Nuremberg Trials after World War ∥. 
    
    Ultimately, this theory connects to many opinions about integrating morality and law. 
    
    Although this assertion seems to be nice and righteous, 
    
    there are serious problems in this. 
    
    Natural law is not specific clause and moral criteria for judging incidents is different for each person. 
    
    If legal professionals considered natural law theory as the basis one,
    
    one of the law’s important elements, objectivity, is disappeared. 
    
    This system is not different with kangaroo court. The more detail problem is mentioned in Hart, H. L. A. (1958). 
    
    According to H. L. A. (1958), faith integrating law and morality can hide essence of the problem we face
    
    and make intellectual confusion for public.
    
    Ultimately, public becomes the condition which can not divide law as it is and as it ought to be. 
    
    If they excessively trust law, our society will be quietism condition which means there are no criticism about law. 
    
    If they excessively distrust law, they will judge recent law is not law, 
    
    and anarchism will be the dominant ideology in our society. 
    
    In other words, contrary to intuitive belief, integrating law and morality can make our society confused
    
    and it is far from essence of law. Through these, we can know necessity of new theoretical approach for realizing ideal law.


## Legal postivism, the perfect theory through ideal law

    Another theory for explaining the essence of law is legal positivism. 
    
    Legal positivism is academic view which just admits positive law as law. 
    
    The strong point of this theory is objectivity. 
    
    It interprets law as just clause and prevents intervention of moral judgement 
    
    which can distort the essence of law through subjectivity. 
    
    In other words, this theory is exact opposite one to natural law theory 
    
    and it completely divides these two ideologies. Some people asserts
    
    this theory can’t reflect our society and make out society righteous,
    
    but this is emotion-biased assertion. When more deeply analyzing this theory, 
    
    we can know this theory means the essence of law. 
    
    One of the thesis which explains this theory perfectly is H. L. A. (1958). 
    
    Hart is one of the giant legal philosopher in 20th century
    
    and he is considered as representative scholar of 20th-century legal positivism. 
    
    He presented this thesis in 1958 as separating theory 
    
    and legal positivism were criticized by public due to impact of Nazi. 
    
    Nazi criminals asserted they just followed the German law at that time, 
    
    so there are no evidences for punishing themselves.
    
    Although they were found guilty in Nuremberg Trials,
    
    public’s distrust about legal positivism remained the same.
    
    So, Hart was written this thesis for changing this atmosphere made by public and legal professionals,
    
    this is evaluated as prestigious thesis. The main concept is core and penumbra, 
    
    admitting the minimum of natural law when establishing law for survival.
    
    First, Hart asserts there are two parts in law.
    
    Core is the certain parts which can make judgement based on law. 
    
    And, penumbra is the uncertain parts which can not make judgement instantly through law. 
    
    In this situation, human’s judgement is necessary. But, this is not application of morality.
    
    Judge just regards law as clause and makes judgements considering previous judicial precedents,
    
    the situation when the law is established etc. 
    
    In other words, it means human’s judgement is just mechanical decision 
    
    for resolving problems made by penumbra parts not moral decision. 
    
    Second, Hart admits the minimum of natural law when establishing law. 
    
    It is for our lives as we will be dangerous if the law can not guarantee our safe. 
    
    But, this permission is applied only for the process of making law. 
    
    And the situation when morality affect legal judgment 
    
    after making law considered as distortion of essence of law. 
    
    Also, Hart asserts moral judgement can be applied when making law 
    
    even if that law is not related to our lives. 
    
    But, this is only for the time when establishing law. Hart presents 2 advantages which legal positivism has. 
    
    They are clarity and sharper foundation for moral criticism. 
    
    These are related to negative things of natural law theory.
    
    First, clarity means it can prevent anarchism and quietism made by intellectual confusion.
    
    Thanks to this trait, public can separate law and morality 
    
    and they can avoid intellectual confusion. Second is related to quietism.
    
    In other words, avoiding quietism through separating theory can increase public’s interest of law 
    
    and can propose new bills based on moral values. These are contexts of Hart’s theory. 
    
    Hart’s one is evaluated as one of the perfect application method of legal positivism to reality.
    
    But, although this is perfect theoretically, there are many problems in reality
    
    such as human judge’s inherent limitations, 
    
    application other incorrect methods related to legal positivism to reality instead of correct methods such as Hart’s one. 
    
    In the following section, we will deal with the problems in reality although legal positivism is the perfect theory through the essence of law.
