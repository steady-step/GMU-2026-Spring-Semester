### Audience Analysis 

My main goal of this project is to make a change of academic, legal and social fields.

For changing this situation, professional’s discussion should be preceded. 

Ultimately, the field where AI judges can be utilized is legal field. So, this project’s main audience is the people related to legal field. 

But, traditional legal professions have a conservative position about AI. 

Admittedly, as a profession, they can point out AI’s weakness such as biased algorithms, legal value. 

But, the main problem is they just tend to focus on its negative impact due to their threat to employment.

According to this article, Israel lawyers strongly oppose AI startup LoFrayer’, which uses AI for making appeal form automatically 

when people scan traffic violation notice. This topic is very confrontational between CEO and lawyers 

as considering the traffic fine, attorneys' fees is relatively expensive.

Although this can be controversial topic, we can know their strong resistance against AI. 

On the contrary, younger generation, law school students hold a more positive view on this. According to this article, 

even if law school students already know not only AI’s necessary but also its danger, 

law school’s education system about AI is insufficient. It shows existing legal academic’s conservation and law school student’s rationality. 

Ultimately, I chose law school students as this project’s audience as leading to change of existing legal professionals is so difficult. 

I expect leading to discussion about AI judges by law school students, 

the future legal professional’s through this project can make a change in academic field and ultimately social field in the long run.
