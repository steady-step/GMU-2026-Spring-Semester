## Legal positivism

First of all, I recommend you to watch this Youtube video for understanding what legal positivism is. 

This theory is a representative theory about considering law as algorithm. 

It analyzes law logically on the premise of the completeness of the law. Also, it separates law and morality completely and 

prioritize positive law than natural law. Positive law means only passed law by legislature.

In contrast, natural law means the unchangeable laws based on human reason and universal reason. 

It applies in every time and every space. Also, natural law theorists argue that this law is not made by human

and it is just originally inherent in space.  In legal positivism, it denies existence of natural law. 

It just prioritize procedure. The best issue related to legal positivism is whether bad law is law. 

Legal positivism theorists argues that bad law is also law. But, about this, there are many controversial issues.

Do you remember the articles I give you before? In this article, there are two examples. 

First one is a famous case where legal positivism was not applied - Nuremberg trials. After World War 2, 

Trials were held for punishing Nazi war criminals. But, the significant legal dilemma arose. 

They asserted that they just follow their German law and it is impossible to punish themselves 

after changing the law due to the principle of non-retroactivity. Their assertion is based on legal positivism. 

But, panel of judges didn’t accept their assertion. Panel of judges adjudicated that natural law is more higher law than positive law.

This example is one of the best examples not to apply legal positivism in court. 

Next, second one is a suggested example in the article about applying legal positivism at Sweden. It is introducing the Sweden minister’s case.

She was arrested for drunk-driving in the southern city of Malmo. She could use her power for resolving this situation,

but ultimately, she resigned her ministerial position. This example is a representative example for applying legal positivism correctly. 

Legal positivism considers law as procedures and guards against subjectivity of law. Due to the traits of legal positivism, 

the punishment can be applied for her excluding subjectivity. Until now, we talked about two opposite examples about legal positivism. 

When summarizing the content so far, the key points of legal positivism are excluding subjectivity, 

and controversial issues about morality and natural law.

## Natural law theory

Prior to introducing this theory, I also recommend you to read this article. If you read this article with the Youtube video 
 
I give you before, you can understand this theory more easier. As I already explain natural law in previous paragraph, 
 
You can easily understand this concept. This theory is representative theory about considering law as morality. 
 
It analyzes law with value-oriented approach on the premise of moral justification. Also, natural law theorists argue that natural law 

should be prioritized over positive law and it can reflect morality in law. In other words, natural law theory is opposite theory

from legal positivism. It can resolve the controversial issue of legal positivism : whether bad law is law. 

But, it also exists another controversial issue : subjectivity. Many law theorists assert the reason 

why nation prohibits private sanction is subjectivity. Following their assertion, if private sanction is allowed in nation, 

people would do private sanction with their each justice and it would cause social collapse. 

Thus, They assert that law is necessary for doing objective role. But, if morality is added in law,

It is hard to avoid negative opinion to them about how law distinguishes itself from private sanction. 

It is a controversial issue. When summarizing the content so far, the key points of natural 

law theory are including morality in law and controversial issues about subjectivity.
